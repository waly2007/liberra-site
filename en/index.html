Features

Your whole trip, in one place.

Tell us where, when, who with and your budget. Liberra takes care of the rest, from the ticket to the plan for each day.

Compared transport

Plane, train or car, in trip order. For flights, real prices found recently, and a tip when leaving on another day is cheaper.

Stays within budget

Hotel, apartment or hostel: Liberra works out what is left after transport and suggests what fits.

Hour-by-hour plan

Sights, meals and travel time between each stop, built around what you love: culture, nature, food, nightlife…

During the trip

The “Today” screen shows what is happening now and when to leave. An optional reminder arrives 15 minutes before each departure.

Travel together

Invite friends with a code. Everyone adds their expenses in real time; the app works out who owes whom.

Don’t forget

A packing list prepared for the country, the season and your activities: documents, money, adapter, seasonal weather.

How it works

Three questions, one trip ready.

  1. Tell us your ideaDestination (or “pick for me”), dates, who is coming and your maximum budget per person.
  2. Pick your optionsTransport, then where to stay: Liberra suggests, you keep what you like. The plan adapts automatically.
  3. Book and goEvery ticket and stay opens on a booking site, with your dates already filled in.

Partners

For affiliate programs.

Liberra is an independent iOS app developed in Geneva, Switzerland. Here is who we serve and how your offers appear in the app.

Audience

  • Travelers from French-speaking Switzerland: solo, couples, groups of friends and families.
  • City breaks in Europe and long-haul trips, departing from Geneva; prices in CHF (EUR, USD, GBP available).
  • App in French; English and German planned before public release.
  • Status: in testing on iPhone, App Store release in preparation.

How your offers appear

  • In context: inside the itinerary and the daily plan, next to the relevant transport, stay or activity.
  • Pre-filled links (dates, places, number of travelers) for a fast booking.
  • A clear notice next to every link: “Partner link: Liberra may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you”.
  • No paid ads on your brand names, no incentivized clicks, no spam, no sale of user data.

Transparency

What we promise.

Honest prices

Flights marked “real price” come from real recent searches; everything else is an estimate, and the app says so. The final price is the one on the booking site.

Free

The app is free. It is funded by small commissions on some partner links, always labeled, at no extra cost to you.

Your data is yours

Servers in Switzerland (Zurich), no data sold. In the app, you can download your data or delete your account at any time. Privacy policy
