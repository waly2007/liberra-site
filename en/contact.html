Contact

Liberra app for iPhone · Geneva, Switzerland

A question about the app, a problem, an idea? Write to us: we answer in French or English, usually within a few days.

contact@liberra.ch

To get a faster answer

  • A problem in the app: tell us what you were doing, what happened, and your iPhone model. A screenshot helps a lot.
  • Your data: you can already download or erase it yourself in the app (“Réglages” tab, then “Mes données”). For any other request, write to us from your account’s email address. See also the privacy policy.
  • Reporting someone: in your trip, open “Groupe”, tap “…” next to the person, then “Signaler”. We check within 24 hours.
  • Partnerships: introduce your company and what you offer travellers.

A booking?

Liberra does not sell tickets or hotel nights. For a booking made on a partner site (flight, hotel, activity), contact that site directly: only they can change or refund it.
