Privacy policy

Updated October 18, 2026 · Liberra app for iPhone (in testing)

This policy explains what data the Liberra app uses, why, and how you can view or delete it. It follows the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, for users in the European Union, the GDPR.

Who is responsible?

Liberra, an independent project based in Geneva, Switzerland. Contact: contact.liberra@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Your trips, your choices (transport, stays), your plan, your wishes, your packing list, your expenses and your settings are stored on your iPhone. Without an account, none of this is sent to our servers.

With an account

  • Data: your email address, your password (encrypted, we never see it), your first name if you add it, your trips and, for shared trips, the members, expenses and settlements.
  • Why: to find your trips on another phone and to share a trip with friends.
  • Where: with our hosting provider Supabase, on servers located in Zurich, Switzerland.
  • Who sees it: you, and the members of a trip you share (they see that trip, your first name and that trip’s expenses).

Services used by the app

To work, the app queries a few services. They only receive what the search needs, never your name or email:

  • Flight prices: Travelpayouts (airports and dates searched), through our server.
  • Sights and restaurants: Geoapify (OpenStreetMap data) and Wikidata (city name), through our server.
  • Exchange rates: Frankfurter (public European Central Bank rates).
  • Destination photos: Unsplash.

As with any internet connection, these services see the IP address of the request.

Partner links

Some buttons (flights, activities, eSIM, luggage storage) open a partner’s website with a tracking ID, which lets Liberra earn a commission if you book. These links are always labeled. Once on the partner’s site, its own privacy policy applies.

What we don’t do

  • No ads in the app, no analytics or tracking tools.
  • We never sell or rent your data.
  • Reminder notifications are scheduled on your phone, without any server.

How long

Your data stays as long as you keep it. You can erase everything at any time; a deleted account is erased from our servers (see below for shared trips).

Your rights

In the app, Profile tab, then “My data”:

  • Download my data: a file with everything the app keeps on your phone.
  • Erase this phone: deletes all app data on your iPhone.
  • Delete my account: deletes your account, the trips you created (for the whole group too) and your membership in other people’s trips. Expenses you added to someone else’s trip stay in their accounts, no longer linked to your account.

For any other request (access, correction, question), write to contact.liberra@gmail.com. You can also contact the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated.
