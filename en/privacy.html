Privacy policy

Updated November 3, 2026 · Liberra app for iPhone

This policy explains what data the Liberra app uses, why, and how you can view or delete it. It follows the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, for users in the European Union, the GDPR.

Who is responsible?

Liberra, an independent project based in Geneva, Switzerland. Contact: contact@liberra.ch.

What stays on your phone

Your trips, your choices (transport, stays), your plan, your wishes, your packing list, your expenses and your settings are stored on your iPhone. Without an account, none of this is sent to our servers.

Your travel notebook and your photos

  • What the notebook holds: the countries and cities you have visited, your finished trips, and for each one your memories (rating, journal, favourite moment, photos).
  • Where: only on your iPhone, even with an account. The notebook is never sent to our servers or to the members of your trips. It is kept per account: if you sign out, your account’s notebook is hidden (it stays on your iPhone); a notebook started without an account joins your account when you sign in.
  • Your photos: the app has no access to your whole photo library. It only receives the photos you pick yourself (memories, notebook background, profile photo). Memory photos and the notebook background are copied into the app’s folder on your iPhone. Only the profile photo is sent to our servers, if you have an account (see below).
  • Images to share: notebook images are made on your phone. They only leave it if you share them yourself (Messages, Instagram…).

With an account

  • Data: your email address, your password (encrypted, we never see it), your first name if you add it, your profile photo if you add one (otherwise the drawn avatar or color you choose), your trips and, for shared trips, the members, expenses, settlements and wishes. If you report or block someone, we also keep that report or block.
  • Why: to find your trips on another phone and to share a trip with friends.
  • Where: with our hosting provider Supabase, on servers located in Zurich, Switzerland.
  • App emails (confirmation code, forgotten password): sent by Resend (Plus Five Five, Inc., a US company based in San Francisco). Emails are sent from Ireland, but your email address and the message may also be processed in the United States, protected by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses adapted for Switzerland.
  • Who sees it: you, and the members of a trip you share (they see that trip, your first name, your photo or avatar, and that trip’s expenses). Your photo is stored in a private folder: nobody else can open it. Nobody sees who reported or blocked whom, except Liberra to handle reports.

Services used by the app

To work, the app queries a few services. They only receive what the search needs, never your name or email:

  • Flight prices: Travelpayouts (airports and dates searched), through our server.
  • Sights and restaurants: Geoapify (OpenStreetMap data) and Wikidata (city name), through our server.
  • Exchange rates: Frankfurter (public European Central Bank rates).
  • Weather forecast: MET Norway (approximate location of the trip’s city).
  • Destination photos: Unsplash.
  • Directions: when you tap “Itinéraire”, Google Maps opens with the address of the place.

As with any internet connection, these services see the IP address of the request. To prevent abuse, our server counts price and place searches per device (a random identifier created by the app) and per IP address; these counters are erased after two days at most.

Partner links

Some buttons (flights, activities, eSIM, luggage storage, airport transfers, delayed-flight compensation) open a partner’s website with a tracking ID, which lets Liberra earn a commission if you book. These links are always labeled. Once on the partner’s site, its own privacy policy applies.

What we don’t do

  • No ads in the app, no analytics or tracking tools.
  • We never sell or rent your data.
  • Reminder notifications are scheduled on your phone, without any server.

How long

Your data stays as long as you keep it. You can erase everything at any time; a deleted account is erased from our servers, profile photo included (see below for shared trips). A report is kept while it is handled, then for one year at most.

Your rights

In the app (in French), “Réglages” tab, then “Mes données”:

  • Download my data: a file with everything the app keeps on your phone, notebook texts included. Photos are not in it: they are copies of your own photos.
  • Erase this phone: deletes all app data on your iPhone, including the notebook and copied photos.
  • Delete my account: deletes your account, the trips you created (for the whole group too) and your membership in other people’s trips. Expenses you added to someone else’s trip stay in their accounts, no longer linked to your account.

For any other request (access, correction, question), write to contact@liberra.ch. You can also contact the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated.
